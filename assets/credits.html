Model credits

Vela X1 concept taxi

Original procedural mesh created for this game. Fictional Vela X1 styling, illuminated wheels and particle thrust effects. No downloaded concept-car asset is included.

Toyota HiAce

Low Poly 2013 Toyota HiAce by Alvin.Woodly, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Adapted for Harare: flattened hierarchy, wheel pivots and animation, ground alignment, paint and window adjustments, and working lamp materials. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

Traffic cars

Compact, sedan and police models from the Cars Pack by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0. Adapted with game-scale dimensions, wheel pivots, paint variations, window materials and working lights. Original license notice.

Playable characters

Stylized African Male by Shockwave7 (@Nilefox), under the Sketchfab Free Standard license (NoAI). Adapted with a skeleton, skin weights and locomotion animations; original textures retained.

African Female Rigged with Mouth Morphs by Dale.Nolan, under CC BY 4.0. Adapted with retargeted locomotion, game scale and material conversion. Original skeleton and textures retained. No endorsement is implied.

Skyweaver is the game's original superhero persona; both appearances share its powers.

Pedestrians and locomotion

Universal Base Characters and Universal Animation Library by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0.

Adapted with shirts, trousers, caps or hair, trainers, reduced textures, skin tones, and idle, walking, jogging and jumping animations. Clothing and colours vary across nearby pedestrians. Original license notices: characters and animations.
